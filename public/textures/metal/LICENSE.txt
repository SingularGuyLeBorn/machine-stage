# Metal PBR texture sources

All texture sets in this directory are from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com) and
are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- Metal009  - brushed steel with scratches (metalness / roughness / normal / color)
- Metal038  - scratched steel
- MetalPlates001 - brushed metal plates
- Metal041B - aged / rusted steel

Downloaded at 1K JPG resolution from the ambientCG `1K-JPG` archives.
